    AETHER

    Adaptive Engine for Thinking, Holograms, Experiments & Reasoning

    A holographic workshop run by a team of AI agents. Up to a quarter-million particles of light form an arc reactor, the Iron Man helmet, engines, the human heart and more. You control them with your hands and your voice, and AETHER hands each request to the right agent.

    1. Snap your fingers to summon the hologram. Make a fist to form the model.
    2. Open your hand slowly to explode it into labelled parts. Close it to put it back together.
    3. Twist to rotate, two hands to zoom, point ☝ at a part to inspect it, pinch 🤏 to pull it out.
    4. Push your palm at the camera to fire a repulsor.
    5. Turn on 🎤 and say “Aether, show me the arc reactor and tell me how it works”, or press J and type. Watch the Hologram and Knowledge agents work in parallel.
